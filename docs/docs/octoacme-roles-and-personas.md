## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
The QA/Testing Lead ensures that deliverables meet quality standards and acceptance criteria before release. They own the testing strategy, coordinate validation work, and verify readiness for production.

### Responsibilities
- Define the test strategy and quality gates for the project
- Coordinate automated and manual testing efforts across features and milestones
- Partner with Product and Engineering to align testing with acceptance criteria
- Track defects, quality risks, and release-readiness status
- Validate feature acceptance and provide sign-off before release

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality features that meet user expectations
- Reduce defects and rework before production
- Maintain confidence in release readiness

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement
- Daily/weekly testing syncs and blocker review
- Release readiness reviews and sign-off meetings

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers to ensure testability and defect resolution
- Aligns with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and business expectations
- Reports quality risks and status to Project Managers and stakeholders
- Coordinates with Release Managers on launch readiness and post-release verification

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
The Technical Lead provides technical direction, guides design decisions, and helps the team manage complexity. They are the escalation point for technical trade-offs, architectural concerns, and risk mitigation.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical designs and architecture decisions
- Set standards for maintainability, scalability, and security
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Mentor team members and support design and code review quality
- Help balance delivery speed with system health and long-term maintainability

### Goals
- Deliver robust and scalable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and avoid avoidable architectural drift
- Support team learning and technical decision quality

### Typical Communication
- Design review discussions and architecture planning
- Code review feedback and technical mentoring
- Risk and escalation conversations with PMs and senior stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Developers on technical implementation and trade-offs
- Supports Product Managers by translating product goals into feasible technical plans
- Works with Project Managers on dependencies, constraints, and timeline impacts
- Provides input to QA/Testing Lead on testability and risk-heavy areas

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
The Stakeholder or Sponsor provides business context, prioritization, and decision-making authority. They represent the broader organizational interest and help ensure the project remains aligned with business value and available resources.

### Responsibilities
- Define business goals, outcomes, and strategic priorities
- Approve scope, resources, and significant trade-offs
- Participate in milestone and decision gates
- Remove organizational blockers and resolve escalated issues
- Communicate project status and business impact to broader leadership

### Goals
- Ensure the project delivers measurable business value
- Maintain alignment between delivery work and organizational strategy
- Support successful decision-making and accountability

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder updates and milestone reviews
- Decision meetings for scope, budget, and priorities
- Escalation and issue resolution as needed

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Aligns with Product Managers on priorities and business outcomes
- Provides strategic direction to Project Managers and delivery leads
- Reviews progress and approves key release or go/no-go decisions
- Works with QA and Release Managers to ensure readiness from a business perspective

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
The Scrum Master or Agile Coach helps the team work effectively, removes blockers, and supports continuous improvement. They facilitate healthy delivery practices and help the team stay focused on value and collaboration.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate agile ceremonies such as planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Help teams identify and remove blockers
- Coach the team on working agreements, flow, and continuous improvement
- Support healthy collaboration and cross-functional alignment
- Assist in tracking process improvements and team effectiveness

### Goals
- Improve team execution and delivery flow
- Create a sustainable, collaborative working environment
- Help the team continuously improve their process

### Typical Communication
- Agile ceremonies and facilitation
- Team coaching and retrospective follow-ups
- Cross-team coordination when blockers affect delivery

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Supports Project Managers by improving team rhythm and execution discipline
- Works with Product Managers to clarify backlog priorities and refine work
- Coordinates with Developers to reduce friction and improve team flow
- Helps release and QA activities run smoothly by improving planning and cross-functional communication

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
The Release Manager coordinates launch planning, deployment readiness, and post-release validation. They help ensure releases are predictable, well communicated, and aligned with operational and stakeholder expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release schedules, sequencing, and deployment windows
- Prepare rollout plans, rollback paths, and communication needs
- Validate release readiness with engineering, QA, and operations
- Manage communication to stakeholders and support teams before and after launch
- Monitor release issues and coordinate response for incidents or regressions

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and deployment disruption
- Improve operational readiness and stakeholder confidence
- Ensure a clear path for rollback or mitigation when needed

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and readiness reviews
- Deployment coordination and stakeholder notifications
- Post-release follow-up and incident communication

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with QA/Testing Lead to confirm readiness and quality gates
- Coordinates with Project Managers on rollout timing and dependencies
- Supports Product Managers with customer-facing communications and milestone alignment
- Partners with Developers and Technical Leads to manage rollback and incident response if needed
