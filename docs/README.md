# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

Welcome to the OctoAcme project management documentation set. This repository contains the core guidance used to plan, execute, track, communicate, and improve work across OctoAcme projects. The goal of these documents is to provide a consistent, repeatable framework for delivery, risk management, stakeholder alignment, and continuous learning.

OctoAcme follows a structured project lifecycle from initiation through retrospective and continuous improvement. Teams begin by validating the business need, aligning stakeholders, and defining a clear one-pager with goals, success metrics, milestones, risks, and responsibilities. After approval, the team moves into planning, where work is prioritized, scoped, estimated, and broken into shippable increments with clear acceptance criteria and dependencies. During execution, progress is tracked with project boards, regular standups, demos, and weekly delivery check-ins so teams can quickly identify blockers and make adjustments.

The process depends on clearly defined roles and responsibilities. Product leads define customer value and outcomes, project managers coordinate scheduling, risk management, stakeholder communication, and documentation, while developers and QA partners focus on delivery, testing, and quality assurance. Communication is intentionally regular and structured: daily team updates, weekly syncs, milestone reviews, and ad-hoc escalations when needed ensure visibility and alignment across stakeholders. Risk management is handled through a shared risk register, escalation paths, and a single source of truth for project status so teams can address issues before they impact delivery.

Quality and delivery assurance are built into the process from planning through release. Teams are expected to write measurable acceptance criteria, keep PRs reviewable, run automated tests and linting in CI, and complete security and smoke testing when appropriate. Release readiness requires validation of acceptance criteria, deployment checks, rollback planning, and post-deploy verification. After each sprint or major milestone, the team conducts a retrospective to capture wins, identify improvements, and convert action items into concrete follow-up work. This creates a continuous learning loop that strengthens execution over time.
